الرجفان الأذيني — Atrial Fibrillation (AF)
What are the primary anticoagulation thresholds based on the CHA₂DS₂-VASc score for non-valvular Atrial Fibrillation in males vs females?
Oral anticoagulation (DOAC preferred) is recommended for CHA₂DS₂-VASc ≥ 2 in males and ≥ 3 in females. It should be considered for a score of 1 in males and 2 in females.
Clinical Pearl: The 'Sc' (Sex category) component adds 1 point for female sex, but female sex alone (score of 1) does not warrant anticoagulation without other risk factors!

High-Yield USMLE / PLAB Clinical Vignettes

A 67-year-old female with permanent Atrial Fibrillation is on Digoxin and Warfarin. She is prescribed Clarithromycin for community-acquired pneumonia. Five days later, she presents with nausea, yellow-tinted vision (xanthopsia), and palpitations. What is the mechanism of this toxicity?
✓ Correct: Option B

Digoxin is primarily eliminated renally via P-glycoprotein (P-gp) efflux pumps. Macrolides (clarithromycin, erythromycin) are potent P-gp inhibitors, leading to acute digoxin accumulation and toxicity (visual xanthopsia + fatal arrhythmias).

📖 Reference: Goodman & Gilman's Pharmacology 14th Ed.
